## Root causes

- **`author-blind-spot-on-own-mutants`** (third occurrence; named in the 2026-10-23 retro, repeated in the 2026-10-25 Rulings 9-11 retro). The lane's mutants and the table both missed the year-0000 output. The fix widened the accepted domain, and nobody probed the new edge of that domain through the public functions until the reviewer did. The implementer saw the edge and judged it out of scope. Cause: the edge only exists because of this lane's change, so it was in scope; "flagged and left" was applied to something the diff had made reachable.
- **A guard whose tests cannot tell it from its removal** (#127 is the residue; `guard-tests-cannot-tell-weaker-guard`, second-to-third occurrence after #34 and #102). Six guards in `safe-to-spend.ts` were deletable with the suite green since w1; they were found by test-guard on PR #96 and filed, and this lane closed them. The cause is upstream of this lane: guards were written without a refusal row each.
- **The boundary-input checklist recommended in the 2026-10-25 Rulings 9-11 retro (change 2, `docs/testing.md` Money) has not landed.** `docs/testing.md` at HEAD has no section on boundary inputs (-0, zero, the other scope, the edges of an accepted domain). That retro called its own recurrence; this lane is another instance, and no artifact changed in between. Cause: the recommendation sat with the retro and had no owner or issue.
- **A ruling's prose was not checked against its own formula** (`ruling-prose-contradicts-formula`, first occurrence; plan seat, `Missed-by: plan` for #128). The final sentence of Ruling 12 is a claim about totals; the formula above it does not support it in one regime. Hannah's text is appended verbatim by rule, so the lane could not correct it, only surface it.
